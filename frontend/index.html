ABOUT CLIPSCUTPRO

More moments. Less manual editing.

Clipscutpro helps creators, educators, and teams repurpose long recordings into short videos. The first version uses speech transcription and a transparent highlight-ranking heuristic; it is a starting point that can later be upgraded with a dedicated semantic highlight model.
